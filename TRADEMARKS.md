# Rohinik Trademark Policy

"Rohinik", "Rohinik Runtime", "RS-1", and the Rohinik logo (when published)
are trademarks of the Rohinik project maintainers.

## Permitted Uses

You may use the Rohinik name to:
- Refer to the Rohinik project, runtime, or architecture in factual, descriptive context
- Describe compatibility or integration with Rohinik (e.g., "compatible with Rohinik RS-1")
- Attribute origin in forks or derivative works under Apache 2.0

## Restricted Uses

You may not use the Rohinik name or logo to:
- Name a competing product or service in a way that implies official endorsement
- Imply official affiliation or certification without going through the RS Certification Program
- Register domain names, social handles, or package namespaces that could cause confusion
  with the official project

## RS Certification Program

Implementations that pass the RS Certification suite (`@rohinik-org/runtime-certification`)
may use the "Rohinik RS-1 Certified" badge. Contact maintainers for details.

## Questions

Trademark inquiries: open an issue in the project repository.
